## Pokémon cries

The classic Generation I cries from the [PokeAPI cries](https://github.com/PokeAPI/cries) repository ("legacy" set),
fetched by `tools/fetch-cries.mjs`. The sounds are © Nintendo / Creatures Inc. / GAME FREAK inc.; used here in a free,
non-commercial fan project. Battle and menu sound effects are synthesised in the browser (no recordings).
